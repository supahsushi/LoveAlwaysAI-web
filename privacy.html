Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Love Always AI is designed to provide a personal companion experience while respecting your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how to contact us.

Information We Collect

The app may collect information you provide directly, such as profile details, companion preferences, conversation content, reminders, special dates, memories, and support messages. If you enable device or account features, the app may also use information needed to provide those functions, such as sign-in identifiers, subscription status, iCloud sync data, or notification settings.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate the app, personalize your companion experience, remember saved details, provide reminders, support subscriptions, improve reliability, and respond to support requests.

Data Storage and Sync

Some information may be stored on your device or synced through Apple services such as iCloud when you enable those features. Third-party services may process data only as needed to provide app functionality, analytics, subscriptions, hosting, or support.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Love Always AI may send reminders or companion-related alerts. You can change notification permissions in your device settings.

Children

Love Always AI is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it when appropriate.

Your Choices

You can update or delete certain information in the app, disable permissions in device settings, cancel subscriptions through Apple, or contact us for privacy questions.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact bentulj4@gmail.com.

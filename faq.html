Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about the app, conversations, memory, reminders, Premium, purchases, and support.

About Love Always AI

What is Love Always AI?

Love Always AI is an iOS companion app built for meaningful conversations, everyday support, reminders, special dates, saved memories, and a personalized companion experience.

What can my AI companion do?

Your companion can chat with you, remember approved details, save special dates, help with reminders, use weather context when you set a location, and support a personalized chat background and companion profile.

Is Love Always AI a real person?

No. Love Always AI is an AI companion, not a real person. Responses are generated by artificial intelligence and should not replace professional advice, emergency help, or real-world care.

Can I personalize my companion?

Yes. You can set your companion's name, choose or generate a background image, and adjust profile traits such as personality, behavior, and appearance descriptions.

Conversations & Memory

What does Memory do?

Memory stores important details you approve, such as preferences, meaningful moments, health notes, birthdays, or events, so your companion can refer to them in future conversations. Free users can save up to 15 memories; Premium unlocks unlimited memories.

What is "Her Mind"?

Her Mind is where you can view short-lived thoughts and saved memories. Thoughts are temporary, while approved memories stay available until you delete them.

How does my companion remember things about me?

There are two ways to save specific things. If it is a birthday, anniversary, event, or important date, tell your companion to remember the date and it can be saved to Special Dates and added to Memory. If it is a lasting personality or behavior detail, add what you want her to remember in her profile under Behavior.

Can I clear saved memories?

Yes. You can open Her Mind, view saved memories, delete individual memories, or clear all memories. Avatar Profile also includes a reset option that clears chat history and memories.

Does my conversation history get deleted?

Yes. To keep the app running smoothly, raw conversation messages are automatically removed after 10 days. This does not affect Memory — anything you've told your companion to remember stays saved (up to your Memory limit). Since chat history doesn't stick around, it's worth telling her to remember anything that matters to you so it's saved to Memory instead of only living in the conversation.

Are conversations synced between devices?

Not yet. Memories, special dates, and reminders are currently stored locally on your device (and conversation history auto-deletes after 10 days regardless). Cross-device backup and restore through your Sign in with Apple account is planned for a future update.

Dates & Reminders

How do Special Dates work?

Special Dates let you save birthdays, anniversaries, goals, and events. You can add them manually, or tell your companion to remember an important date so it can go into Special Dates and Memory. The app can show how many days are left and can treat birthdays or anniversaries as recurring yearly dates.

Can Love Always AI remind me about birthdays or important events?

Yes. Birthday dates can schedule birthday notifications, and reminders can create local notifications for the time you choose. Some reminder and date tools require Premium.

Why didn't I receive a reminder?

Check that notifications are allowed for Love Always AI in iPhone Settings, that the reminder time has not passed, and that your device is not blocking alerts through Focus or notification settings. Premium-only reminders also require an active Premium subscription.

How do I edit or delete reminders?

Open the Reminders screen from the chat controls. You can remove active reminders there, which also cancels the related notification.

What does Premium include?

Premium includes unlimited conversations, deeper conversations, custom reminders and wake-up notifications, special dates tracking, long-term memory, custom location and live weather, and priority responses.

How do I subscribe?

Open Love Always AI, go to your companion profile, tap the button that says Love Always AI Pro, choose Monthly or Annual, and confirm the purchase through the App Store.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. The app uses Apple's StoreKit restore flow to reconnect active App Store subscriptions.

I paid but Premium isn't showing. What should I do?

First tap Restore Purchases in the app. Then check iPhone Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions, and confirm Love Always AI is active. If it still does not show, contact support at bentulj4@gmail.com.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iPhone Settings, tap your Apple ID, choose Subscriptions, then select Love Always AI to manage or cancel. Premium access continues until the end of the current billing period.
